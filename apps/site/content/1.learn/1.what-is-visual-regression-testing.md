---
title: What is visual regression testing
description: A screenshot of your UI, compared pixel by pixel against a known-good one, so that unintended visual changes fail the build.
navigation:
  icon: i-lucide-eye
---

Visual regression testing takes a screenshot of a page, a component or a screen, and compares it with a baseline image that was approved earlier. If the two differ by more than a threshold, the test fails and the diff is kept for a human to look at.
